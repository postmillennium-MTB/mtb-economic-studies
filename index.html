Data sources & methodology notes
Visitor spending: TPL/IMBA Economic Benefits of Mountain Biking (2025), 50-study meta-analysis. Conservative $103/trip = Duluth, MN; moderate $175/trip = regional avg; optimistic $416/trip = TPL national avg.

Property value premiums: Crompton & Nicholls (2019); NAR Field Guide (2020). Typical: 3–5%. Indianapolis Cultural Trail: $1B added within 500 ft, 2008–2014.

Trail build & maintenance costs: Singletracks.com contractor survey (Apr 2026); American Trails database ($1,500–$5,000/mi/yr). Highway costs: FHWA / DOT data (2020–2024). Jobs: IMBA ~$45,000 labor income per FTE.

Local vs. out-of-town: Out-of-town visitors bring new money — full multiplier applied. Local visitors recirculate existing spending — multiplier = 1.0. Per Savolt (2017) Duluth study and Buning & Lamont (2021).

Grants: RTP (Recreational Trails Program) up to 80% · RAISE · CMAQ · state programs. Grant % reduces municipal capital contribution for ROI and breakeven calculations.

Units & currency: All research benchmarks are USD/imperial. Metric and foreign-currency displays are conversions for directional reference only. Exchange rates fetched daily from fawazahmed0/currency-api (jsdelivr CDN); fallback rates used if unavailable.

Breakeven: Year when cumulative municipal tax revenue ≥ municipal share of construction cost. Maintenance excluded from breakeven denominator.

This tool is for planning and advocacy. Validate assumptions with local data before formal presentation.
